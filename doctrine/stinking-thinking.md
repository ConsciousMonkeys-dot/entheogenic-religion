# Stinking Thinking

## The Main Purpose of the Movement

The main purpose of this movement is to remove the stinking thinking from all the people of Northern Ireland.

## What Stinking Thinking Is

Stinking thinking is the habit of putting people down in speech:

- "Your man, sure he's an idiot."
- "He's no good."
- "She thinks she's great, but she's not great."

It is a nasty way of speaking about others that is common in Northern Ireland — a small place where everyone knows everyone, and where anyone who rises above the crowd risks being knocked back down. This instinct has a name: **begrudgery**.

## Why It Matters

The spoken word is spellcasting. What you speak shapes what you become. "He's an idiot" plants a seed in the speaker's own mind as much as it lands on the target. The put-down becomes the speaker's reality.

The person who says "she's no good" is really saying "I don't believe good is possible" — and that belief feeds back into their own life.

## The Fix

The same mechanism that creates the put-down can create the blessing. "I am a good man" plants a different seed than "I'm not good." The word spoken outward meets the voice heard inward — and that is where the meeting happens.

The movement does not fight stinking thinking with more stinking thinking. It replaces it with spoken truth: family, sovereignty, the inner voice that says you're going to be okay.

## The Call

Every voice that stops putting people down and starts speaking what is true is one more voice singing the new vision into reality.
