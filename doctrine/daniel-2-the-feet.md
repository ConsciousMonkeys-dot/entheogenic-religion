# Daniel 2: The Feet of Clay

## The Statue

Nebuchadnezzar's dream in Daniel 2: a great statue with a head of gold, chest of silver, belly of bronze, legs of iron, and feet of iron mixed with clay.

The statue is the succession of empires: Babylon the golden head, Medo-Persia the silver chest, Greece the bronze belly, Rome the iron legs, and the mixed feet the final kingdom.

## The Feet Are the People

The feet are the only part of the statue that touches the ground. The gold head, the silver chest, the iron legs — none of it stands without the feet holding it up.

And the feet are the weakest part. Iron mixed with clay does not bond — it crumbles. The whole structure rests on the least stable material, which is the people.

## The Movement

The moment the feet walk away, the statue falls. Not because the people fought it — because they stopped holding it up.

That is the sovereignty doctrine in one picture: the system only has the power you hand it, and the power was never in the head. It was in the feet all along.
