# The Ladder: Better Than

## The Question

Northern Ireland raises its children on the ladder: "What are you going to be when you grow up?" "Are you going to make it?" "Have you made it?"

What is "make it"? If you make it, are you better than somebody?

## The Trap

The minute you think you are better than somebody, you have already admitted that somebody else is better than you. The ladder has no top — it only has people above and people below, and you are always both.

The ladder is the same mechanism as stinking thinking, pointed at yourself. The put-down of others and the climb past others are one system: comparison. And comparison is the fuel the fear economy runs on.

## The Fix

The religion does not climb the ladder. It steps off it.

The vision is not "better than" — it is "enough." The inner voice that says "you're going to be okay" does not say "you're going to be better." It says you are already whole.

The movement removes stinking thinking from the people of Northern Ireland — and the ladder is where stinking thinking starts, in the nursery.
