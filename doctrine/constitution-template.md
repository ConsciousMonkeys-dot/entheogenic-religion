## 1. Name

The name of the movement shall be **[NAME]**, hereafter referred to as "the Movement."

## 2. Registered Office

The registered office of the Movement shall be at **[ADDRESS]**, or such other place as the Trustees may determine.

## 3. Objects

The objects of the Movement are:

(a) To advance religion, specifically the entheogenic faith as set out in the Movement's core beliefs, which holds that:

- The inner voice, the vision, and the nudge are the faculty of spirit within every person;
- The plant medicine is a sacrament that opens the door to consciousness, and the integration work afterward is the healing;
- The spoken word is spellcasting — what we say shapes what we become;
- God is vibration, and the throat is the bridge between the God outside and the God within;
- All people are equal, each part of God having their own experience;
- The main purpose of the Movement is to remove stinking thinking from the people of Northern Ireland;
- The people are the feet of clay in Daniel's vision — the system stands only because we hold it up, and we may walk away.

(b) To promote the healing of trauma through consciousness, teaching that the wound lives in the mind and the healing must happen there;

(c) To build community through shared practice: the questionnaire, the songs, the empty-cup work, and regular gatherings.

## 4. Powers

In furtherance of the objects, the Movement may:

(a) Hold meetings, ceremonies, and gatherings;
(b) Publish and distribute written, audio, and video materials;
(c) Raise funds by lawful means;
(d) Employ staff and engage volunteers;
(e) Acquire and dispose of property;
(f) Do all such other lawful things as are necessary for the attainment of the objects.

## 5. Membership

(a) Membership is open to any person who supports the objects of the Movement and agrees to abide by this constitution.

(b) Members shall pay such subscription as the Trustees may determine.

(c) The Trustees may refuse or terminate membership where a member acts against the objects or brings the Movement into disrepute.

## 6. Trustees

(a) The Movement shall have a minimum of three Trustees.

(b) Trustees shall be appointed by **[the existing Trustees / the members at a general meeting]**.

(c) Trustees shall serve for a term of **[three years]**, renewable.

(d) A Trustee may resign by written notice to the other Trustees.

(e) The Trustees shall meet at least **[quarterly]**.

## 7. Meetings

(a) An Annual General Meeting shall be held once in each calendar year.

(b) A Special General Meeting may be called by the Trustees or by **[ten percent of members]**.

(c) Notice of at least **[fourteen days]** shall be given for any general meeting.

(d) Decisions at general meetings shall be by simple majority of those present and voting.

## 8. Finance

(a) The financial year shall end on **[31 March / 31 December]**.

(b) Accounts shall be kept and presented to the Annual General Meeting.

(c) The Movement shall maintain a bank account in its name.

(d) No Trustee shall receive payment for their services, except reasonable out-of-pocket expenses.

## 9. Dissolution

If the Movement is dissolved, any remaining assets shall be transferred to **[another charity with similar objects / as the Trustees determine]**, and not distributed to members.

## 10. Amendments

This constitution may be amended by a two-thirds majority at a general meeting, provided notice of the proposed amendment is given with the meeting notice.
