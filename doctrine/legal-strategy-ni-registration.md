# Legal Strategy: Registering as a Recognized Religion in Northern Ireland

## The Short Answer

There is no separate "religion register" in Northern Ireland. The path is to register as a **charity** with the Charity Commission for Northern Ireland, where "advancing religion" is one of the twelve recognized charitable purposes. The law defines religion broadly: belief in one god or more than one god, or belief in a supreme being or beings.

## The Process

1. Submit an **Expression of Intent** form on the Commission's website.
2. The Commission calls you forward within about six weeks.
3. Complete the online application: governing document (constitution or trust deed), trustee details, bank details, and a trustee declaration.
4. Assessment takes three to five months.

## The Religious Designation Catch

**Religious designation** — the special status that gives extra protections — requires:

- Five years established in Northern Ireland
- Regular public worship as your principal activity
- Internal disciplinary authorities

So the strategy is: register as a charity now, then apply for designation once you've been running five years.
