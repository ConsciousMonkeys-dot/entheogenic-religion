# Practice

This folder holds the daily practice of the entheogenic religion — the practical side of the faith, where doctrine becomes habit.

## The Two Documents

**[The Herbal Medicine Menu](herbal-medicine-menu.md)** — *what* to drink.

Every herb in the tradition, organized by what it does for the body: pain, gut, immune, blood sugar, mood, sleep, liver and kidneys, skin, and parasites. It includes how to prepare each tea, the weekly routine, and the safety checklist.

**[The Daily Tea Liturgy](daily-tea-liturgy.md)** — *how* to drink it.

The sacramental practice that turns a remedy into devotion: the intention before the first sip, the preparation as prayer, the drinking as receiving, and the closing. The liturgy does not change day to day — only the herbs change.

## How They Work Together

The menu is the map. The liturgy is the journey. Together they form one practice: the body tended, the mind clear, the vessel ready for gnosis.

Start with the menu to learn the herbs and build your weekly rhythm. Then let the liturgy shape how you receive each cup. The intention line — *"I drink this as medicine, not as escape"* — is the seed of the whole practice. Everything else can be learned; that sentence is what you pass on first.
