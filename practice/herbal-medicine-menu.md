# The Herbal Medicine of the Entheogenic Tradition

## Introduction

This document is the practical medicine manual of the entheogenic religion. The tradition holds that God created plants as medicine — that the same creation that gave us frankincense, myrrh, and gold also gave us clove, ginger, and turmeric. This is not alternative medicine replacing doctors. It is the daily practice of the faith: tending the body as a temple, using what the earth provides, with intention and respect.

This menu tells us *what* to drink. The companion document, [The Daily Tea Liturgy](daily-tea-liturgy.md), tells us *how* to drink it — turning a remedy into a sacrament.

## Why

The body is the vessel of consciousness. A clear body supports a clear mind, and a clear mind is the doorway to gnosis. The herbs in this tradition have been used for thousands of years across cultures — in the UDV, Santo Daime, Rastafari, Sufi, and Hindu traditions — not as drugs, but as sacraments and medicines. The active compounds in these plants are real, studied, and effective. This document organizes them by what they do, how to prepare them, and when to use them.

## How to Make the Teas

**Resin teas (frankincense, myrrh):** Use food-grade resin only — never incense-grade. Use about one teaspoon of crushed resin per cup. Steep in hot water for fifteen to twenty minutes, or simmer gently for ten. The resin is hard and bitter; a longer steep extracts more.

**Whole spices (clove, Ceylon cinnamon):** Two to three whole cloves or one to two cinnamon sticks per cup. Steep ten minutes. You can reuse the same cloves for a second cup.

**Fresh roots (ginger, turmeric):** Slice about a thumb-sized piece. Steep ten minutes. For turmeric, always add a pinch of black pepper and a splash of milk or coconut oil — curcumin is fat-soluble and piperine boosts absorption up to twenty times.

**Dried herbs (chamomile, nettle, thyme, etc.):** One to two teaspoons per cup. Steep five to ten minutes, covered.

**Cold infusions (marshmallow root, slippery elm):** Soak one tablespoon in cold water overnight. Drink in the morning. Heat destroys the mucilage.

**Powders (moringa, maca, turmeric):** One teaspoon stirred into hot water or a smoothie.

**Spruce or fir needle tea:** A handful of fresh needles, steeped ten minutes. Know your species — ponderosa pine and yew are toxic.

## Each Herb and What It Does

**Frankincense (food-grade resin)** — Boswellic acids. Anti-inflammatory; strongest evidence for joint pain and osteoarthritis. Traditional for gut inflammation and digestion. Used in meditation as a calming tea.

**Clove** — Eugenol. Pain relief, antiseptic, digestion, blood sugar. Kills parasite eggs and larvae. Blood thinner — occasional use only.

**Ceylon cinnamon** — Cinnamaldehyde. Modest blood sugar reduction, anti-inflammatory, antioxidant. Use Ceylon only — cassia contains coumarin, which stresses the liver.

**Myrrh (resin)** — Furanoeudesma-1,3-diene and curzerene. Oral health, antimicrobial, gut inflammation. Traditional partner to frankincense.

**Turmeric** — Curcumin. Anti-inflammatory, joint pain, digestion, low mood. Needs black pepper and fat to absorb. Blood thinner.

**Peppermint** — Menthol. Nausea, headache, bloating, congestion. Avoid if you get reflux — use spearmint instead.

**Ginger** — Gingerols. Best-evidenced herb for nausea of any kind. Migraine, muscle pain, warming, congestion. Safe daily.

**Rosemary** — Rosmarinic acid, camphor. Memory, alertness, antimicrobial. Safe daily.

**Pine needle (spruce or fir)** — Vitamin C, shikimic acid. Immune support, traditional scurvy remedy. Know your species.

**Chamomile** — Apigenin. Calming, sleep, mild gut antispasmodic. Caution with ragweed allergy.

**Lemon balm** — Rosmarinic acid. Anxiety, sleep, antiviral for cold sores. Safe daily.

**Valerian root** — Valerenic acid. Sleep, anxiety. Do not combine with sedatives.

**Lavender** — Linalool. Anxiety, sleep, headache. Safe daily.

**Holy basil (tulsi)** — Eugenol, ursolic acid. Stress, immune, blood sugar. Safe daily.

**Green tea** — EGCG, L-theanine. Antioxidant, alert calm, metabolism. Contains caffeine.

**Nettle** — Histamine, silica. Allergies, joints, kidneys, iron. Safe daily.

**Dandelion** — Inulin, potassium. Liver, digestion, diuretic. Caution with ragweed allergy.

**Milk thistle** — Silymarin. Liver protection. Safe daily.

**Echinacea** — Alkylamides. Cold prevention, immune. Avoid if autoimmune.

**Elderberry** — Anthocyanins. Cold and flu duration. Raw berries are toxic — use syrup or tea only.

**Yarrow** — Achilleine. Fever, bleeding, digestion. Avoid in pregnancy.

**Sage** — Thujone. Sore throat, memory, menopause. Not daily long-term.

**Thyme** — Thymol. Cough, antimicrobial, gut. Safe daily.

**Fennel** — Anethole. Bloating, gas, milk supply. Safe daily.

**Anise** — Anethole. Cough, digestion. Avoid in pregnancy.

**Licorice root** — Glycyrrhizin. Sore throat, adrenal, gut. Raises blood pressure.

**Marshmallow root** — Mucilage. Sore throat, gut lining. Cold infusion.

**Slippery elm** — Mucilage. Gut lining, reflux, throat. Cold infusion.

**Ginseng** — Ginsenosides. Energy, stamina, blood sugar. Not with blood thinners.

**Ashwagandha** — Withanolides. Stress, cortisol, sleep. Avoid in pregnancy.

**Maca** — Macamides. Energy, hormones, libido. Safe daily.

**Moringa** — Vitamins, quercetin. Nutrition, inflammation. Safe daily.

**Hibiscus** — Anthocyanins. Blood pressure, liver. Caution with blood pressure medication.

**Rooibos** — Aspalathin. Antioxidant, gentle, no caffeine. Safe daily.

**Wormwood (Artemisia)** — Artemisinin. Strongest antiparasitic evidence — Nobel Prize for malaria. Short courses only; neurotoxic in excess.

**Black walnut hull** — Juglone. Traditional vermifuge, kills worms and eggs. Short courses only.

**Pumpkin seeds** — Cucurbitacin. Paralyzes worms so the body expels them. Safe daily.

**Garlic** — Allicin. Antimicrobial, some antiparasitic. Safe daily.

**Oregano oil** — Carvacrol. Potent antimicrobial. Diluted drops only; short courses.

**Papaya seeds** — Carpaine. Traditional antiparasitic. Blend into smoothies.

**Berberine herbs (goldenseal, barberry)** — Berberine. Antimicrobial, some antiparasitic. Not with certain medications.

**Neem** — Azadirachtin. Traditional antiparasitic in Ayurveda. Not in pregnancy.

## Illness Categories

**Pain — joints, muscles, headache:** Frankincense, turmeric, clove, peppermint, ginger.

**Gut, digestion, bowel:** Ginger for nausea, peppermint for cramps and bloating, clove and myrrh for gut inflammation, frankincense for digestion, fennel for gas.

**Immune, cold, flu:** Spruce or fir needle for vitamin C, ginger for congestion, rosemary and thyme as antimicrobial, clove and myrrh for throat and mouth, elderberry and echinacea for cold duration.

**Blood sugar:** Ceylon cinnamon has the best evidence, clove and turmeric help modestly, holy basil and ginseng support insulin sensitivity.

**Mood and mind:** Rosemary for memory and alertness, peppermint for headaches, turmeric as a low-mood adjunct, frankincense as a traditional calming tea, ashwagandha and holy basil for stress.

**Sleep:** Chamomile, lavender, lemon balm, valerian.

**Liver and kidneys:** Milk thistle, dandelion, nettle.

**Skin:** Frankincense and myrrh oils diluted in carrier oil, turmeric topically — it stains yellow.

**Parasites:** Pumpkin seeds daily as the base, clove tea plus black walnut hull as a one-to-two-week course then a break, wormwood only under guidance. Cut sugar and refined carbs — parasites feed on them. If you have real symptoms — GI issues, fatigue, unexplained weight loss — get a stool test first. Herbs alone won't clear an actual infection.

## Safety Checklist

- Blood thinners: clove, turmeric, and frankincense — don't stack all three on the same day.
- Reflux: swap peppermint for spearmint.
- Pregnancy: avoid wormwood, yarrow, anise, ashwagandha, and neem.
- Autoimmune: skip echinacea.
- Blood pressure medication: caution with licorice and hibiscus.
- Sedatives: no valerian.
- Symptoms like GI issues, fatigue, or weight loss: stool test before parasite self-treatment.
