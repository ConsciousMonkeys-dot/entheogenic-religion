# David Luke: Complete Works Dossier

**A comprehensive research document on the career, publications, and research of Professor David Luke (University of Greenwich), compiled for the entheogenic religion project.**

*Compiled October 2026. Sources: Psi Encyclopedia, Google Scholar, University of Greenwich staff page, ORCID, ResearchGate, publisher pages, and Luke's own Substack. Where chapter-level detail of books is available it is included; where it is not, the book is described at the level of its documented contents.*

## 1. Who David Luke Is

David Luke is Professor of Exceptional Experience at the University of Greenwich, London, where he has worked since 2008. He leads the **Psychedelic and Exceptional Experience Lab (PEEL)**, established within the Centre for Mental Health in late 2025. He is also a Perrott-Warrick Senior Researcher (administered via Trinity College, Cambridge) studying lucid dream precognition, and was Honorary Senior Lecturer at Imperial College London's Centre for Psychedelic Research from 2019 to 2023.

Career highlights:
- First-class degree in psychology, University of Westminster; PhD from the University of Northampton, including parapsychological research.
- President of the Parapsychological Association, 2009–2011.
- Co-founder (2010) and trustee of **Breaking Convention**, the UK's leading academic conference on psychedelic consciousness; chair 2019–2022.
- Founder (2008) of the Ecology, Cosmos and Consciousness salon at the Institute of Ecotechnics, London.
- Lecturer on the MSc Consciousness, Spirituality and Transpersonal Psychology (Alef Trust / Liverpool John Moores University) and programme leader for the professional certificate in Psychedelics, Altered States and Transpersonal Psychology (Alef Trust).
- Has given over 400 invited public lectures and conference presentations.
- Awards: Early Career Research Excellence Award (Greenwich, 2011); faculty's first Inspirational Teaching Award (2016).

He has published **more than 100 academic papers and around thirteen books** in the field of transpersonal experiences, anomalous phenomena, and altered states of consciousness, especially via psychedelics. He coined the term **psychedelomancy** — the use of psychedelics to improve psi ability.

His stated hope for his work: to help lead humanity towards embracing a more psychologically liberated, ecologically aligned, and richer reality.

## 4. Major Journal Articles and Papers

### DMT and entity encounters
- Luke, D. (2011). "Discarnate entities and dimethyltryptamine (DMT): Psychopharmacology, phenomenology and ontology." *Journal of the Society for Psychical Research*, 75(902), 26–42. - Luke, D. (2012). "Psychoactive substances and paranormal phenomena: A comprehensive review." *International Journal of Transpersonal Studies*, 31(1), 97–156. (~74 citations.)
- Luke, D. (2005, with M. Kittenis). "A preliminary survey of paranormal experiences with psychoactive drugs." *Journal of Parapsychology*, 69(2).

- Luke, D. (2022). "Anomalous psychedelic experiences: At the neurochemical juncture of the humanistic and parapsychological." *Journal of Humanistic Psychology*, 62(2), 257–297.
- Luke's DMT fieldwork: parapsychological testing reportedly produced higher psi scoring under DMT than in an ordinary state (p = 0.048; twenty volunteers per condition) — reported in Psi Encyclopedia; full paper details to be confirmed.

Reported in talks (e.g., 2019 YouTube "Psychodelomancy") and Psi Encyclopedia; not yet in a peer-reviewed journal.

## 6. How Luke's Work Maps to the Entheogenic Religion Project

Luke's corpus is unusually well-suited to a project that treats plant medicines as sacraments and consciousness-expanding technologies, because he is one of the few academics who takes the *religious and anomalous dimensions* of the experience seriously rather than reducing them to neurochemistry alone. Key threads for the project:

1. **DMT as a doorway to other minds/worlds.** His 2011 paper, the *DMT Dialogues* and *DMT Entity Encounters* symposia, and the Michael/Luke/Robinson field studies all document that a majority of high-dose users encounter apparently sentient nonhuman presences — beings that users across cultures describe as wise, loving, and instructive. Luke's own framing ("entheogenic plant sentience") treats the plant as potentially a conduit between spirit and matter.

2. **The brain-as-filter model.** In *Otherworlds* (Chapter 9) Luke reviews psychedelic/neurochemical models of paranormal experience, including the idea that DMT and related tryptamines may open the filter rather than create the experience — a framework that resonates with entheogenic theology's claim that these substances reveal rather than fabricate.

3. **Psi under psychedelics (psychedelomancy).** His DMT fieldwork (higher psi scoring, p = 0.048), San Pedro experiments (p = 0.032), and dream-precognition circadian findings (stronger at 3 am when melatonin peaks) suggest these states may enhance access to information beyond ordinary perception — directly relevant to claims of gnosis or divine communication.

4. **Ecodelia and ecological conversion.** The 2023 *Transpersonal Ecodelia* survey (Irvine, Luke, Harrild, Gandy, Watts) found near-universal increases in nature connectedness and majority dietary/behavioural change — evidence that these sacraments reorient the user toward the living world, a core ecological-theological claim.

5. **Creative and scientific insight.** The unpublished LSD scientist trial and the Gandy et al. 2022 review document breakthroughs in theoretical and technical problem-solving — supporting the claim that these medicines expand not just mystical but intellectual capacity.

6. **Near-death-like states.** The 2025 PLOS ONE comparison shows DMT replicates NDE features, including encounters with death — linking the sacrament to the oldest religious questions about mortality and what lies beyond.

7. **Ontological shock and integration.** Luke's recent work on ontological shock (Argyri et al. 2025) and integration pathways (Adams, Michael & Luke 2026) acknowledges that these experiences destabilize reality-models — which, framed religiously, is precisely what initiation does.

## 7. Important Caveats for Project Use

- Luke's most striking claims (the scientist-trial breakthroughs, the DMT psi finding) rest on small samples and, in the trial's case, are not yet peer-reviewed. Use them as *indicative*, not conclusive.
- The LSD precognition result was below chance — Luke interprets this as skeptical scientists "using" psi to deny psi, but alternative explanations (order effects, small N) are live.
- Luke is a parapsychologist by training and inclination; mainstream neuroscience does not accept psi. The dossier records what he found and claimed; it does not adjudicate the metaphysics.
- Several of his books are edited anthologies: the chapter authors (Strassman, McKenna, Narby, Sheldrake, Metzner, Bache, Kripal, etc.) are distinct voices, not Luke's own positions.
- Where this dossier lists chapter titles, they are taken from publisher pages or the Academia.edu full-text preview of *Otherworlds*; for books whose contents are not publicly indexed, only titles and descriptions are given.
